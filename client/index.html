How VibePune Works

We bridged high-performance PostGIS spatial algorithms with generative NLP so you discover places that genuinely fit your mood.

01

Spatial Indexing & GIS

Over 8,139 Pune landmarks and local venues verified via OpenStreetMap, with PostGIS GiST R-Tree indexing for sub-10ms ellipsoidal distance queries.

ST_DWithin • PostGIS
02

Natural Vibe Matching

Our 5-step questionnaire uses Google Gemini 1.5 Pro to extract semantic intent from natural descriptions, backed by a 100% resilient deterministic fallback.

Gemini 1.5 Pro NLP
03

Multimodal Routing

Real turn-by-turn road geometries calculated on-the-fly via OSRM for walking and driving, coupled with seamless Google Maps navigation handoff.

OSRM Engine • Turn-by-Turn
